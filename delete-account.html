Delete Your SurgiLog Account

Two ways to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

1
Open SurgiLog and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2
Go to Account, scroll to the Danger Zone.
3
Tap Delete My Account, confirm your password, and confirm the deletion.

This immediately and permanently deletes your login, your case log, and your profile information from our servers. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — No longer have the app installed?

Email a deletion request from the address on your SurgiLog account, and we'll delete your account and data manually.

Include in your email:

  • The email address your SurgiLog account uses
  • The subject line "Delete my SurgiLog account" (or similar)
Email tma4866@gmail.com

Requests are processed as soon as possible, and always within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your login credentials, display name, qualifications, profile photo, and every case in your logbook (including any attached photos) are permanently removed from our cloud database. This is a full deletion, not a deactivation — the data is not recoverable afterward.

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for more detail on how your data is handled.
