SurgiLog

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

SurgiLog is a personal surgical case logbook app, built and operated by Dr. Tariq Mehmood ("I", "me", "the developer"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it. It applies to the SurgiLog app on both Android and iOS.

What information SurgiLog collects

When you create an account, SurgiLog collects:

  • Your email address (used to sign in)
  • A display name, if you choose to set one

When you log a case, SurgiLog stores whatever you choose to enter into the app, which may include:

  • Date, patient age range, and gender
  • Diagnosis and procedure performed
  • Specialty, technique, and case type
  • Your role in the case, and the supervising or assisting consultant's name
  • Hospital and operating theatre
  • Anaesthesia type, and any free-text notes you add
  • A photo you optionally attach to a case, if you choose to add one

SurgiLog does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, or any usage-tracking data, and does not display ads.

A note on patient information: SurgiLog is designed to record de-identified, case-level details for training and logbook purposes — for example, an age range and gender rather than a patient's name or date of birth. Please don't enter patient-identifying information (full names, medical record numbers, exact addresses, or similar) into free-text fields such as Diagnosis or Notes. The app and this policy are built around the assumption that no patient-identifying data is stored.

How your information is used

Your data is used solely to provide SurgiLog's core functionality: keeping your own case logbook, and syncing it across your own devices when you're signed in. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

Where your information is stored

Case data is stored locally on your device, so the app works offline, and is synced to Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication) — Google Cloud infrastructure — when you're signed in and connected. Photos you attach to a case are the one exception: they are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to Firebase or anywhere else. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Firebase is the only third party your data is shared with, and it acts purely as infrastructure: it does not use your data for its own purposes. You can read Google's privacy policy for details on how Firebase handles data on its platform.

Your control over your data

From within the app (Settings), you can at any time:

  • Edit or delete any individual case
  • Delete every case in your logbook, locally and in the cloud
  • Permanently delete your account — this removes your login and every case you've logged, with no recovery

None of these actions require contacting support — they're available directly in the app. If you'd prefer to make a request by email instead, see the contact details below.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, associated data is removed from Firebase; a local copy may remain on a device until the app is reinstalled or its local storage is cleared, but it will no longer sync or be accessible through your (now-deleted) account.

Children's privacy

SurgiLog is a professional tool intended for surgeons and surgical trainees. It is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, for example as the app gains new features. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
